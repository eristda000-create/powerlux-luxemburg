# 7 Nov 2026 — Social + Sponsor Inventory

**Status:** planning inventory; no sponsor promises; no rights assumptions
**Format assumption:** approx. 20 pre-arranged Supermatches, approx. 60 total people

## Core commercial idea

Do not sell the event as one logo placement. Sell **repeatable media inventory generated by 20 matchups**.

Every Supermatch can become:
- pre-event matchup reveal;
- athlete profile/story;
- face-off or quote card;
- live/result asset;
- short decisive-moment clip;
- full/replay segment where rights permit;
- post-event result card;
- sponsor-tagged recap unit.

This creates many more sponsor impressions than the room capacity alone suggests.

## Suggested sponsor package logic

Do not publish fixed prices until venue/production economics are known.

Use 4 layers:
- **Supporter:** small logo + social thank-you;
- **Match Partner:** one matchup + result/highlight inventory;
- **Event Partner:** repeated event/social/on-site presence;
- **Title Partner:** dominant event identity + largest media package.

Price floors should be calculated from:
- hard event/production cost;
- expected audience and digital reach;
- number of deliverables;
- exclusivity/category rights;
- athlete/story rights;
- production effort;
- whether partner provides cash or real cost-offset value.
