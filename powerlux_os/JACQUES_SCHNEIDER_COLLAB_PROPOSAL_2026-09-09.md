# Jacques Schneider × PowerLux — Collaboration Proposal Draft

**Status:** proposal draft; not sent; no partnership claim
**Date:** 2026-09-09

## Core idea

**Make Luxembourg's under-recognised athletes visible through sport, art and national identity.**

PowerLux exists to give niche sports and athletes more visibility, better storytelling and concrete commercial opportunities. Jacques Schneider's public work repeatedly connects Luxembourg identity, local creation and social meaning. His current support of powerlifter Emma Weydert shows that the overlap with under-recognised strength sports is already real, not theoretical.

## Why the fit is unusually strong

- Jacques Schneider already supports a Luxembourg niche-sport athlete.
- Public coverage around that collaboration explicitly discusses the visibility/funding problem of smaller sports and names arm wrestling among the overlooked disciplines.
- PowerLux can provide the missing **media/event/distribution layer** around that same issue.
- Jacques brings cultural legitimacy, distinctive visual language and Luxembourg identity.
- PowerLux brings athletes, events, sport stories, content and PowerTV distribution potential.

## Three concrete collaboration formats

### Option A — Hidden Champions Luxembourg
A recurring portrait/content series featuring under-recognised Luxembourg athletes.

Each episode/asset can include:
- athlete portrait;
- short story: sport, achievement, sacrifice, next goal;
- Jacques-designed visual element / signature treatment;
- PowerLux athlete profile;
- PowerTV short feature / interview / event clip when source and rights allow;
- sponsor/partner layer only where authentic and approved.

**Benefit:** scalable beyond armwrestling into powerlifting, strongman, combat sports, motocross, Hyrox and other niche sports.

### Option B — 7 November 2026 Supermatch activation
Use the Supermatch/Vendetta event as a visible first pilot.

Possible activation elements:
- limited event poster / key visual;
- athlete matchup cards;
- photo wall / media backdrop;
- one designed trophy/award or symbolic object;
- limited numbered print or merchandise piece;
- Jacques/PowerLux social co-post around the event mission;
- athlete portraits or short `why this athlete matters` stories.

This option is attractive because it is **specific, time-bound and easy to evaluate**.

### Option C — Athlete Support Capsule
A small ethical/local product or print capsule where a defined share supports athlete travel/entry/training costs.

Important: economics, production, IP, beneficiary selection and charity/tax framing must be agreed before any public claim.

## Suggested first ask to Jacques

Do **not** ask for a broad sponsorship immediately.

Ask for a 20–30 minute meeting around one concrete question:

> Could we build a first pilot together that makes Luxembourg's overlooked athletes visible — ideally around the 7 November Supermatch event — and then decide from the result whether it should become a recurring series?

## Meeting agenda

1. 2 min — PowerLux mission and current niche-sport problem.
2. 3 min — Why Jacques specifically fits; reference his existing niche-sport support respectfully.
3. 5 min — Show Option A/B/C visually/conceptually.
4. 5 min — Ask what he would genuinely enjoy creating, rather than forcing a pre-defined sponsorship package.
5. 5 min — agree smallest pilot, responsibilities, rights and timing.

## What PowerLux should bring

- one-page PowerLux overview;
- verified athlete/event examples;
- 7 Nov event facts / venue status clearly labelled verified vs pending;
- moodboard only as inspiration, not fake finished artwork;
- proposed deliverables and who produces what;
- social/media distribution plan;
- clear rights/usage discussion before publishing artwork.

## Success criteria for first pilot

- one jointly approved creative output;
- measurable athlete/event reach;
- earned media or partner interest;
- reusable content asset;
- no ambiguous IP/brand rights;
- both parties want a second activation.
