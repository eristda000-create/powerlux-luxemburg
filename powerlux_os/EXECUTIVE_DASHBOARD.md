## 30-day target set from current PowerLux playbook
- **3–5** pilots

- **2+** written references

- **48h** event review after each appearance/event

## Daily executive order of operations
1. **Revenue/proof first:** who can buy, partner or pilot now?
2. **Remove blockers:** price, contract, insurance, permit, website reliability, owner capacity.
3. **Execute next actions:** calls, proposals, pilot prep, follow-ups.
4. **Record evidence:** CRM, payment, agreement, KPI, screenshot, review.
5. **Only then expand:** new feature, sport, product or market.

## Escalation rules
Escalate immediately to Sean + Tom when a proposed action involves:
- binding contract or strategic commitment;
- ownership/IP transfer;
- material spending outside approved guardrails;
- new safety/liability exposure;
- legal/tax/TVA ambiguity affecting a real sale/event;
- public partner/claim that cannot be evidenced;
- production change that can materially break the live website.

## Weekly review format
Answer only six questions:
1. What **actually changed** this week?
2. What generated or moved closest to **cash/repeatable proof**?
3. What is blocked and by whom?
4. Which assumption was proven wrong?
5. What do we stop doing next week?
6. What are the three P0 actions before the next review?
